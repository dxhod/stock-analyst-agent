# Portfolio Disclaimer

Portfolio Builder output is informational and educational only. It is not personalized financial, investment, tax, legal, or accounting advice.

Users should evaluate their own financial situation, objectives, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, tax status, and investment restrictions before acting on any allocation.

Market data can be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect. Scoring models are simplifications and cannot predict future returns. Diversification does not guarantee profit or protect against loss.

The portfolio should be reviewed periodically and rebalanced when allocations drift materially from target weights or when the investment thesis changes.
