# Sector Notes

Technology can provide strong growth and margin profiles, but portfolios with heavy technology exposure may become sensitive to valuation compression and interest-rate expectations.

Healthcare can add defensive growth, recurring demand, and innovation exposure. Risks include regulation, patent cliffs, trial failures, and reimbursement pressure.

Financial Services can benefit from higher rates, credit growth, and capital markets activity. Risks include credit cycles, regulation, liquidity events, and yield-curve pressure.

Communication Services includes digital advertising, media, social platforms, and telecom. It can provide growth and cash flow, but may be sensitive to ad cycles and regulation.

Consumer Cyclical can perform well when consumers are strong, but it is more exposed to recessions, discretionary spending declines, and margin pressure.

Consumer Defensive can reduce volatility because demand is less cyclical. Upside may be lower during risk-on markets.

Industrials can provide economic-cycle and infrastructure exposure. Risks include order cycles, input costs, and global demand.

Energy can hedge inflation and commodity shocks, but earnings are often tied to oil and gas prices.

Utilities and Real Estate can add income and defensiveness, but they may be sensitive to interest rates.

Semiconductors can provide structural AI and compute exposure, but the industry is cyclical and can be valuation-sensitive.

Broad Market ETFs can reduce single-name risk and provide diversified core exposure.
